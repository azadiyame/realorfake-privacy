# Real or Fake — Help & Support

Need help? Email **[support@adooba.com](mailto:support@adooba.com)** and we'll get back to you. Please include your device model and iOS version.

## How it works

1. Tap **Choose from Photos**, **Browse Files** or **Paste from Clipboard**, or share any file to Real or Fake from another app.
2. The App reads the file's hidden metadata on your device: camera make and model, lens and exposure, GPS location, capture and edit dates, software names, XMP edit history, Content Credentials (C2PA), IPTC AI labels, PNG generation parameters, PDF properties and revision history, Office document authorship and more.
3. You get a verdict — **Looks authentic**, **Edited**, **Probably AI-generated**, **AI-generated** or **Inconclusive** — with a confidence percentage and the list of clues behind it.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why is the result "Inconclusive"?**
The file carries almost no metadata. This is normal for images received through WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram or other apps, which strip metadata when sending. Ask the sender for the original file if you need a stronger answer.

**Can the App prove a photo is fake?**
It finds the fingerprints that AI tools and editors leave behind. When a file contains a Stable Diffusion prompt, an IPTC "trained algorithmic media" label or signed Content Credentials from a generator, that is definitive. When metadata was removed, the App can only report what is missing. Always combine the result with your own judgement and the source of the file.

**Are my files uploaded?**
No. Everything runs on your iPhone or iPad. See the [Privacy Policy](index.html).

**What does Pro include?**
Unlimited checks for photos, PDFs, videos and documents. Your first checks are free. Pro is a monthly auto-renewing subscription managed through your Apple Account.

**How do I restore my subscription?**
Open **Settings → Restore purchases**. Pro is restored on any device using the same Apple Account.

**How do I cancel?**
Open the iOS **Settings** app → your name → **Subscriptions** → Real or Fake Pro → Cancel.

**Which files are supported?**
JPEG, HEIC, PNG, WebP, GIF, TIFF and RAW photos; MP4, MOV, M4A and MP3 media; PDF; Word, Excel and PowerPoint (.docx/.xlsx/.pptx), OpenDocument, Pages/Numbers/Keynote packages, RTF, plain text and zip archives.
