# Real or Fake — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 23 October 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how **Real or Fake** ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our iOS and iPadOS app.

## The short version

**Real or Fake does not collect any personal data.** Every file you check is analysed **entirely on your device**. Your photos, videos, PDFs and documents are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs.

## 1. Files you analyse

When you choose a photo, video, PDF or other file, the App reads its embedded metadata (for example EXIF camera data, GPS location, dates, software names, XMP edit history, Content Credentials and document properties) **locally on your device** to produce a report. The file and the report stay on your device only. A copy of the report and a small thumbnail are stored in the App's private storage so you can revisit it in History; you can delete them at any time from History or Settings.

The App never transmits your files, their metadata, their location data or the analysis results to our servers, because we have no servers.

## 2. Location data

Some photos and videos contain GPS coordinates. The App shows this location to you on a map and may ask Apple's geocoding service on your device to turn the coordinates into a place name. This request is handled by iOS under Apple's privacy policy. The App does not access your device's current location.

## 3. Photo library access

The App uses the system photo picker, which only gives the App access to the individual items you select. It cannot browse your library.

## 4. In-app purchases

Real or Fake offers an optional auto-renewing subscription, **Real or Fake Pro**. All payments are processed by **Apple** through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment details. The App checks Apple's signed transaction information on your device only to unlock Pro features and to restore them with the "Restore purchases" button.

## 5. Advertising and tracking

Real or Fake shows **no ads** and does **not** track you across apps or websites. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).

## 6. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Because we do not collect data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Reports stored on your device can be deleted from History, and all App data is removed when you delete the App.

## 8. Accuracy disclaimer

The App's verdicts are based on the metadata embedded in files. Metadata can be removed or altered, so a verdict is evidence, not absolute proof. Use your own judgement and consider the source of a file.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
